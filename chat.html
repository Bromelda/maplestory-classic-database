Chat filter Early test

Keeps buddy, party and general chat apart while you play, so a buddy's message isn't lost in a busy town. Share your game window and it reads the chat box as lines come. Speakers (megaphones) and other chat are left out.

Sharing a window needs Chrome or Edge (or Firefox) on a computer.

Text

How to use it

  • Press Share game window and pick the MapleStory window (not your whole screen). Keep the chat box showing. Open it (Enter) to catch more lines at once.
  • Pop out on top opens a small chat window that stays over the game. Right-click any tab (Buddy, Party, General) to open it in a window of its own, so you can watch two or three at once.
  • Keep this page open while you play. It can sit behind the game, but don't minimise it: browsers slow down pages that are minimised.
  • Words are read from the picture, so now and then a letter comes out wrong, and a line over busy scenery can be missed.

Everything happens in your browser: the picture and the chat never leave your computer, and they're gone when you close the page. It only looks at the picture. It never touches the game or plays for you. Use at your own risk: we couldn't find a clear Nexon rule on tools that read the screen, so check their rules before using it on an account you care about.
